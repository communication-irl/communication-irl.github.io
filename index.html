Communication for Interactive Robot Learning

Full-day workshop at HRI 2027

Location: Santa Clara, CA, USA

Workshop Overview

Recent advances in foundation models, robot learning, and learning from human data have created new opportunities for robots to acquire increasingly generalizable skills. However, robots operating in human environments cannot rely solely on large-scale offline datasets or predefined task specifications. They must be able to learn new behaviors, adapt existing skills, and resolve uncertainty through continued interaction with people.

In this interactive learning process, communication between humans and robots is essential: humans need to convey their intentions and knowledge, while robots need to communicate their uncertainties and learning progress.

Aligned with this year's HRI theme of “Innovative HRI”, this workshop explores how communication for interactive robot learning is evolving alongside advances in robotics and artificial intelligence. As robots become increasingly capable of understanding diverse human inputs, reasoning about their own learning needs, and actively engaging with human teachers, new opportunities emerge for how humans and robots can teach and learn from one another.

Through these discussions, we aim to identify new forms of communication and interaction that can shape the next generation of interactive robot learning.

Call for Papers

Topics of Interest

We encourage researchers and students from human-robot interactions, robot learning, robotics, artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and related disciplines to contribute to this workshop. We welcome a broad range of submissions, including but not limited to the following topics:

  • Interactive robot learning
  • Learning from human demonstrations
  • Learning from language and multimodal instructions
  • Learning from human feedback
  • Novel interfaces and modalities for robot teaching
  • Active learning and information seeking
  • Question asking and clarification
  • Communicating uncertainty, capabilities, and learning progress
  • Safety and responsible interactions
  • Human factors in interactive robot learning

Submission Guidelines

We welcome submissions describing completed research, work in progress, position papers, system demonstrations, and other contributions relevant to the workshop.

Submissions should follow the HRI 2027 full paper formatting guidelines, using the ACM Proceedings template (available both from ACM and on Overleaf). We accept extended abstracts (2 pages), short papers (4 pages), and full-length papers (8 pages); all page limits exclude references.

This workshop is non-archival, meaning accepted papers will not appear in archival conference proceedings, and authors are free to submit their work to future conferences and archival venues. All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review and receive two reviews from our program committee, so authors should ensure that their manuscripts are properly anonymized.

Around five accepted papers will be selected for spotlight talks (8 minutes plus 2 minutes of Q&A), and the remaining papers will give lightning talks (2 minutes). All accepted papers are also invited to present in the poster session, providing an opportunity for discussion, feedback, and networking with other participants.
